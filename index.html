About Me

I am Jiahao Xie, an undergraduate student in Clinical Medicine at Dalian Medical University. My current academic interests lie at the intersection of computational chemistry, bioinformatics, molecular modeling, and translational biomedical research.

I work with molecular dynamics, quantum chemistry, network pharmacology, network toxicology, Mendelian randomization, GBD database analysis, WGCNA, differential analysis, KEGG/GO enrichment analysis, molecular docking, and machine-learning-assisted target screening. I am also involved in wet-lab work including cell culture, medium change, passage, cryopreservation, QPCR, CCK8 assays, and a small part of animal-experiment support.

Research

My research aims to use computation and biomedical data analysis to form mechanistic hypotheses that can be evaluated experimentally.

  • Computational Chemistry: Molecular Dynamics with GROMACS; quantum chemistry workflows with ORCA and Gaussian; molecular analysis with Multiwfn, sobTop, and Molclus.
  • Bioinformatics: Network pharmacology, network toxicology, Mendelian randomization, GBD database analysis, WGCNA, differential analysis, KEGG/GO enrichment analysis, molecular docking, and machine-learning-assisted target screening.
  • Experimental Validation: Cell culture medium change, passage, cryopreservation, QPCR, CCK8 assays, and limited animal-experiment support.
  • Scientific Computing: Reproducible workflows for molecular modeling and computational chemistry practice, including work connected to MAPLE.
