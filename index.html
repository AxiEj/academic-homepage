Research Interests

My academic interests center on using molecular-level computation and biomedical data analysis to generate mechanistic hypotheses that can be tested experimentally.

Computational Chemistry

Molecular Dynamics with GROMACS; quantum chemistry workflows with ORCA and Gaussian.

Bioinformatics

Network pharmacology, molecular docking, and machine-learning-assisted target screening.

Experimental Validation

Cell culture, QPCR, CCK8 assays, and animal-experiment support for biomedical projects.
